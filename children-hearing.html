Children’s hearing care for Cambodian families

Calm, family-centred support when parents, teachers, or caregivers notice listening, speech, learning, or attention concerns.

Children’s hearing care Cambodia

Early understanding can support communication and learning.

Children may not always explain hearing difficulty. Families may notice speech delay, unclear speech, frequent requests for repetition, turning one ear toward sound, high TV volume, or difficulty following instructions in noise.

Children’s appointments should be gentle, age-aware, and clear for caregivers. If medical or specialist paediatric referral is needed, families should receive understandable guidance.

Signs to watch

When to ask about a child’s hearing

Speech and language

Delayed speech, unclear words, or reduced response to spoken instructions may need attention.

School and listening

Difficulty hearing teachers, following group conversations, or listening in noise can affect learning.

Ear health history

Repeated ear infections, ear pain, or drainage should be reviewed by suitable health professionals.

Concerned about your child’s hearing?

Use the booking form with your child’s age, concern, and any known ear-health history. Emergency or painful ear symptoms should be reviewed promptly by a qualified medical provider.
