Hearing care services

Professional, family-friendly support for hearing assessment, technology, counselling, tinnitus education, and ongoing device care.

🩺

Hearing assessment

A hearing test Cambodia appointment may include discussion of symptoms, ear history, listening needs, and suitable hearing checks. Results are explained clearly so you understand what they mean for daily life.

  • Adult and older-person hearing checks
  • Clear result explanation
  • Guidance on next steps and referrals if needed
👂

Hearing aid fitting

When hearing aids are appropriate, fitting should be personalised to hearing results, comfort, budget, phone use, work, family life, and listening priorities.

  • Device selection guidance
  • Fitting and fine-tuning
  • Realistic adaptation counselling
🔔

Tinnitus support

Tinnitus can feel stressful. Support focuses on education, ear-health guidance, listening strategies, sound management ideas, and referral when medical review is needed.

  • Easy-to-understand education
  • Coping and listening strategies
  • No unrealistic cure claims
🔧

Hearing aid maintenance

Good device care keeps hearing aids working more reliably. Maintenance visits can include cleaning, troubleshooting, wax-filter education, earmould checks, and accessory guidance.

  • Cleaning and listening checks
  • Battery or rechargeable care
  • Practical daily-care training
🤝

Family counselling

Hearing care works best when families understand how to help. Counselling can cover speaking clearly, reducing background noise, creating good listening routines, and supporting confidence.

  • Family communication strategies
  • Support for older people
  • Practical home and work tips
🧒

Children’s hearing care

Children need calm, age-aware care and family-centred explanations. Visit the children’s page for signs to watch for and appointment guidance.
